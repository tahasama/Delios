# Running DELIOS: known gaps

What the console, payments and legal pieces (docs/RUNNING-DELIOS.md, "Built") do not do yet.

- **The end of a contract**: an organization set to Ended reads only; its data is exported by its administrator as one
  package (the full download, `/full-download`), deleted after the agreed time by the app owner or whoever of our team
  they delegate, managed internally (owner, 30 Oct 2026). Both are owed by the data processing agreement; the number of days is
  the lawyer's to write.
- **CMI and Paddle** are not connected: invoices, their PDFs and payments recorded by hand are built; a card payment
  through either is recorded on its invoice by hand until its merchant contract and keys exist.
- ~~**Renewal notice 30 days before**~~: done, told by itself (notification and email).
- **Operators** are added and reset by the backend command only; switched off and on from the console's Team (done).
- **Support is a conversation** (done): back and forth inside the app; a reply by email to the support address is still
  not taken in.
- **Choosing a plan** shows its price before it is sent (done); setting the plan in the console may issue the first
  invoice at once (done); later invoices are issued by hand, each period.
- **Organizations that signed up before the free trial existed** have no end date until one is set on their page in
  the console.
- **Codes**: confirmed by the owner on 30 Oct 2026 (subscription, payment, invoice and support kinds); the legal matters'
  states (TO_DO, PLACEHOLDER, WITH_LAWYER, IN_FORCE, NOT_NEEDED) are still to confirm.
