# Schedule & actions — surface brief

Scope: Schedule & actions (/actions) and the action page (/actions/[code]). Mode: Operate. Users: Document Control at a desk on a big screen, long sessions; planners; discipline engineers confirming. Trial of a new visual world on these two pages only; if it fails, fall back to "same look, better layout". Owner's complaint: generic, everything looks the same.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Site dockets. The two pages read as the carbon-copy forms every project runs on: a printed layer (labels, rules) in graphite, the filled layer (values) in carbon-blue ink, and state as a rubber stamp. Refuses the category default of grey cards with one blue accent where every element weighs the same.

OWN-WORLD: White top-copy sheets on a cool off-white ground; sheet heads on a canary copy band; pink copy tint for what needs someone; carbon-blue ink for values, links and the primary button; graphite print for labels and rules, square-cornered boxed fields with a corner label. Narrow grotesque (Archivo Narrow) for printed labels and headings, Archivo for values and text. Stamps: red overdue (double border), amber at risk (dashed), green ready (solid), slate postponed (struck), each slightly turned.

STORY: The planner sees at once which counts are pink and which actions carry a loud stamp, opens one, and reads it as one filled-in docket: the name large in ink with its stamp beside it, the facts in boxed fields, the documents it needs in the table below, each discipline's confirmation signed on a ruled line.

FIRST VIEWPORT: /actions: sheet head on the canary band with "Schedule & actions" and the in-force date; under it the stage path, where the dates come from, and five boxed fields (documents ready, went ahead with documents missing, no date, disciplines tagged, discipline lists), pink where somebody is needed; the three upload buttons to the right; filters; then the plan, and in the table each state is a stamp. /actions/[code]: canary head with the way back, the action name large in ink and the large state stamp to its right; four boxed fields under it (action No., day of the work, documents ready, disciplines confirmed); the documents table below.

FORM: Site dockets (carbon-copy forms), position 5 of the grounded list, seed key 1edae805. Raises: state by shape as well as colour (from the emission-line rail); few type sizes, rank by weight and case (from the timetable rack); one fact leads on the action page (from the feed surface).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
