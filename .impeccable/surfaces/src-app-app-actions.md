# Schedule & actions — surface brief

Scope: Schedule & actions (/actions) and the action page (/actions/[code]). Mode: Operate. Users: Document Control at a desk on a big screen, long sessions; planners; discipline engineers confirming. Trial of a new visual world on these two pages only; if it fails, fall back to "same look, better layout". Owner's complaint: generic, everything looks the same.

## Decision

Two new looks were tried on these pages on 10 Oct and both rejected by the owner: "Site dockets" (carbon-copy forms: disliked colours, paper feel, narrow fonts, heaviness) and "Planning board" (petrol band, own faces, board view). The original look stays; work on these pages is refinement (polish) of the incumbent style only. Do not propose a new visual world for them unless the owner asks.
